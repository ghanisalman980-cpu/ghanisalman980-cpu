## A. Executive verdict

**Pursue two niches, in this order. Hold a third. Reject the rest.**

### Niche 1 (lead): Senior mining & minerals engineering recruitment into Saudi Arabia — "Western mining engineers and EPCM project leaders for the Kingdom's mining build-out"

- **Economics:** Perm at 25% clears the five-figure-fee gate for genuinely senior roles (exploration manager/geologist ~£63k ⇒ ~£15.8k; senior HSE £47.5–76k; project directors / lead-principal engineers £83–131k; Western expat packages price well above local aggregator medians — tax-free + 40–60% allowances). The £300–400/day contract margin is **plausible but publicly unproven** for KSA mining (no published day rates exist; the case rests on adjacent Gulf senior-contractor benchmarks) — validate against Bechtel/Hatch/Worley contractor bill rates before committing to it. Sector scale is hard fact: Ma'aden's 10-year **$110bn** capital programme ("largest capital programme in the history of mining," 8 megaprojects); new exploitation licences **+220% YoY** in 2025; Bechtel's SAR 391.1m Ar Rjum gold EPCM (Oct 2025); Hatch's portfolio-wide Ma'aden delivery partnership (Jul 2026); ~200k mining jobs targeted by 2030. `[MARKET]` `[INFERENCE]` — full table in §B.
- **SERP gap: REAL.** Live page one for KSA mining-recruitment queries is templated SEO landing pages (Hire Resolve ×2 domains), aggregators (Jooble, Naukrigulf), blue-collar manpower sites, one strong regional generalist (TASC), one boutique exec player (Ruya), and a genuine global mining specialist (Globe 24-7) ranking with a **generic EMEA page, no KSA substance**. No result matches "Western senior mining engineers into KSA, contract + perm." `[SERP]`
- **Why now:** demand is anchored to Riyadh (Ma'aden HQ) and EPCM contractors' in-Kingdom teams; the query space is young enough that page one is still beatable by a legitimate specialist page. `[INFERENCE]`

### Niche 2 (parallel): High-ticket oil & gas contract + perm staffing for KSA — led by drilling/wells leadership and senior EPC project leadership

- **Economics:** the £300–400/day contract margin is **evidenced only at the top of the market** — drilling supervisors/superintendents (senior pay $1,050–1,400/day), senior completions/well-intervention ($1,020–1,380/day), senior reservoir consultants (to ~$1,510/day), senior EPC PMs/project-controls ($883–1,300/day). The 25% perm fee clears £15k+ across almost every senior family (drilling £21–27k, completions £28k+, reservoir £22k+, EPC PM ~£22k, HSE manager £13–30k, senior rotating equipment £24k+; project directors average ~SAR 45k/mo ⇒ ~£26.8k fee). **Failures matter as much:** I&C, QA/QC below manager level, and average-grade process/commissioning contracts do **not** carry the margin — the offer must stay senior/scarce. `[MARKET]` `[INFERENCE]` — full table in §B.
- **Sub-niche 2a — drilling/wells leadership (promoted after cross-validation, §J):** the strongest contract-margin family *and* a job-board/crewing-agency-only SERP (Libera, Fratelli Cosulich, OceanCrew, oiljobia vacancy posts; no employer-side specialist landing page). Scope strictly to superintendent/manager/senior-engineer level — generic "drilling recruitment" drags toward rig-crew manpower economics. `[SERP]` `[MARKET]`
- **Sub-niche 2b — commissioning/start-up leadership (supporting pillar, perm-led):** real *positioning* gap (see §J for the correction to the external report's overclaim), but economics are perm-only: senior perm fees pass (~£16.6k at the SAR ~28k/mo average; ~£29.7k at the SAR 50k/mo ceiling), while the KSA commissioning *contract* market is regional-hire priced (evidenced SAR 7–8k/mo pre-commissioning posts; AED 25–28k/mo lead roles) and cannot carry a £300–400/day margin. `[SERP]` `[MARKET]`
- **SERP gap: REAL on the exact money terms.** "oil and gas recruitment agency saudi arabia" page one is local high-volume manpower agencies (Prime Resources, Yaqoot, Safwa, Teleport, Soundlines, AJEETS), one global generalist (Adecco KSA), and a regional exec-search firm. **NES Fircroft — the strongest specialist — does not appear top-5 on the exact head term** (it ranks for "engineering recruitment" and "Riyadh" variants). Nobody on page one offers "senior Western engineers, contract/day-rate + perm, into KSA oil & gas." `[SERP]`
- **Constraint:** this niche is real only at the **senior/scarce** end. The moment the offer drifts toward generic "manpower supply," it collides with dozens of licensed local suppliers on their home turf and fails the economics filter. `[INFERENCE]`
