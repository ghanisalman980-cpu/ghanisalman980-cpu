## A. Executive verdict

**Pursue two niches, in this order. Hold a third. Reject the rest.**

### Niche 1 (lead): Senior mining & minerals engineering recruitment into Saudi Arabia — "Western mining engineers and EPCM project leaders for the Kingdom's mining build-out"

- **Economics:** Senior mining/EPCM roles (mine managers, EPCM project directors/managers, process-plant managers, senior metallurgists, project-controls leads) are scarce, expat-heavy, and project-driven — the profile that supports both a £300–400/day contract margin and five-figure-GBP perm fees. See §B. `[INFERENCE]` pending final rate evidence; sector scale is hard fact: Ma'aden is a top-10 global miner by market cap executing a doubling of gold output by 2030; Bechtel signed a SAR 391.1m (~USD 104.3m) EPCM contract for the Ar Rjum gold mine on 22 Oct 2025; Hatch signed a strategic delivery partnership with Ma'aden in July 2026. `[MARKET]`
- **SERP gap: REAL.** Live page one for KSA mining-recruitment queries is templated SEO landing pages (Hire Resolve ×2 domains), aggregators (Jooble, Naukrigulf), blue-collar manpower sites, one strong regional generalist (TASC), one boutique exec player (Ruya), and a genuine global mining specialist (Globe 24-7) ranking with a **generic EMEA page, no KSA substance**. No result matches "Western senior mining engineers into KSA, contract + perm." `[SERP]`
- **Why now:** demand is anchored to Riyadh (Ma'aden HQ) and EPCM contractors' in-Kingdom teams; the query space is young enough that page one is still beatable by a legitimate specialist page. `[INFERENCE]`

### Niche 2 (parallel): High-ticket oil & gas contract + perm staffing for KSA — senior/scarce disciplines only

- **Economics:** senior O&G contractors (commissioning managers, drilling/wells seniors, process/I&C/rotating leads, HSE managers, project managers) are the classic day-rate band where a £300–400 margin is realistic; senior perm salaries in KSA support 25% fees well into five figures GBP. See §B.
- **SERP gap: REAL on the exact money terms.** "oil and gas recruitment agency saudi arabia" page one is local high-volume manpower agencies (Prime Resources, Yaqoot, Safwa, Teleport, Soundlines, AJEETS), one global generalist (Adecco KSA), and a regional exec-search firm. **NES Fircroft — the strongest specialist — does not appear top-5 on the exact head term** (it ranks for "engineering recruitment" and "Riyadh" variants). Nobody on page one offers "senior Western engineers, contract/day-rate + perm, into KSA oil & gas." `[SERP]`
- **Constraint:** this niche is real only at the **senior/scarce** end. The moment the offer drifts toward generic "manpower supply," it collides with dozens of licensed local suppliers on their home turf and fails the economics filter. `[INFERENCE]`

### Niche 3 (hold / phase 2): Renewables (solar/wind/hydrogen) senior staffing for KSA

- Leap29 already has a dedicated, competent KSA renewables page; NES Fircroft and WRS have Middle-East renewables pages; ACWA Power hires through a Korn Ferry RPO and direct channels. `[SERP]` The gap is **narrower** and the buyer side is RPO-dominated at scale. Keep as a later hub page once niches 1–2 are established; do not lead with it. `[INFERENCE]`

### Explicit "do not force it" list

- Generic "engineering recruitment agency saudi arabia" head term — page one held by NES ×2, Leap29, Progressive with genuine KSA substance. No gap. `[SERP]`
- Generic "executive search riyadh / energy" — contested by Talent Arabia, Leap29, NADIA, JOH Partners, The Energists, Heidrick. No gap for us. `[SERP]`

- Subsea-for-KSA — regional subsea demand centres on UAE/Qatar offshore; KSA-specific subsea employer demand is thin and the SERP is served by UAE offshore specialists. Reject for KSA targeting. `[SERP]` `[INFERENCE]`

## G. Risks
