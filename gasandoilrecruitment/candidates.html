Before you apply offshore

Most North Sea roles require a valid OGUK medical, BOSIET/FOET with CA-EBS, and MIST. Well-control roles need current IWCF or WellSharp certification. If any of yours have lapsed, tell us — we can often point you to fast-track renewal providers.
