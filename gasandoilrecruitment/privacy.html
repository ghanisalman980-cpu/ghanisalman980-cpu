Privacy notice

How we handle your personal data, in plain English. Last updated September 2026.

Who we are

Gas & Oil Recruitment is a recruitment consultancy based in Aberdeen, Scotland. For the purposes of UK data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018), we are the "data controller" for the personal data you give us.

What we collect

  • Contact details — name, email address and phone number, when you register, apply for a role or contact us.
  • Career information — your CV, work history, qualifications, certifications and right-to-work status.
  • Client enquiry details — company name, role requirements and contact details when you brief us on a vacancy.
  • Website usage data — anonymised analytics (pages visited, approximate location, device type) via Google Analytics 4 and Cloudflare Web Analytics.

How we use it

  • To match candidates with suitable vacancies and present candidates to clients — only with your permission first.
  • To respond to enquiries and manage our relationship with you.
  • To meet legal and compliance obligations (right-to-work checks, tax and payroll law).
  • To understand how the website is used so we can improve it.

Who we share it with

We share candidate details with prospective employers only after telling you. We use a small number of processors under contract: website hosting (Cloudflare), analytics (Google), and our email and applicant-tracking providers. We never sell personal data.

How long we keep it

Candidate records are kept while you're actively working with us and for up to 24 months afterwards, unless you ask us to delete them sooner or the law requires longer retention (for example, payroll records).

Your rights

You can ask us at any time for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, or to restrict how we use it. Email hello@gasandoilrecruitment.com and we'll respond within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Cookies

We use a small number of cookies for analytics only. No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies are set by this website.

Contact

Questions about this notice or your data: hello@gasandoilrecruitment.com, or write to us at Prime Four, Kingswells, Aberdeen AB15 8PU.
