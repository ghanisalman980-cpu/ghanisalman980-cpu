Recruitment by people who know the industry

Gas & Oil Recruitment is an independent consultancy based in Aberdeen — Europe's energy capital — placing certified professionals across the North Sea and in energy hubs worldwide.

Our story

Built on the quayside, not in a call centre

We started Gas & Oil Recruitment because too much of energy recruitment had become a numbers game: CVs scraped by keyword, candidates submitted without a phone call, certificates checked after mobilisation instead of before.

We do it the other way round. Every consultant on our team recruits one discipline — drilling, subsea, production, engineering, HSE or decommissioning — and sticks to it. They know the assets, the operators, the certification regimes and the going rates, because it's all they do.

Fifteen years on, we support operators, drilling contractors and service companies from our Aberdeen base, with over a hundred contractors deployed at any time across the North Sea, the Middle East and West Africa.
