Our vetting standard

Every candidate, checked before you see them

  • Certification verified at source — BOSIET/FOET, OGUK medicals, IWCF/WellSharp, OPITO, CompEx and trade papers checked against issuing bodies.
  • Two recent references from supervisors on comparable assets, taken up before submission.
  • Right-to-work and compliance screening documented and auditable for every placement.
  • Availability confirmed — we only submit people who can actually start when you need them.

If a placement doesn't work out in the first two weeks of a contract, we replace them at no additional fee.
